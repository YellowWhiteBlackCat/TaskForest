# Asset provenance and license

The SVG geometry in `assets/icons/` was created specifically for TaskForest
from basic geometric primitives. No third-party icon files or generated emoji
artwork are included.

The icons are distributed under the repository's Apache-2.0 license. They use
only `currentColor`, have no embedded fonts, scripts, external references,
metadata, raster images, or fixed palette colors.

Bundled fonts in `assets/fonts/` are third-party typefaces with their own
licenses — see `assets/fonts/LICENSE.md` for provenance and per-font terms
(MiSans VF and Roboto Mono are both SIL OFL 1.1; each font's license text
ships beside it and must accompany redistribution).
